# Arabic caption style guidance — v1

- Right-to-left rendering, correct contextual letter shaping (no disconnected glyphs).
- Numerals: default to the dialect region's convention (Eastern Arabic ١٢٣ for
  Gulf, Western 123 for Egypt/Levant) — make this a per-brand setting, not hardcoded.
- Keep caption lines short (readable on a 9:16 mobile screen at a glance).
- Word-by-word highlight timing should follow the transcript's word-level timestamps.
